# Layout catalog

Choose one primary preset. Combine two only when the slide still has one obvious reading order.

## Data metric cards

Use for 2-4 headline measures with comparable importance.

- Fields: short label, value with unit, optional comparison or interpretation.
- Layout: one row for 2-3 metrics; 2x2 grid for four metrics; dominant metric plus a supporting row when one measure clearly leads.
- Keep numeric precision consistent and preserve signs, denominators, dates, and comparison periods.

## Research question cards

Use for 2-4 distinct questions, hypotheses, or study aims.

- Fields: number or short category, question title, one-line scope or method cue.
- Layout: two columns for two questions, three columns for three, 2x2 for four.
- Phrase questions directly. Do not convert unresolved questions into findings.

## Project status cards

Use when the content describes workstreams, milestones, or deliverables.

- Fields: workstream, current state, evidence of progress, next checkpoint.
- Layout: two or three columns for peer workstreams; a dominant progress statement with a compact milestone strip when chronology matters.
- Show status with text first. Use color as reinforcement, not as the only signal.

## Comparison table

Use when at least three items share the same attributes or exact values need lookup.

- Keep the row or column orientation that minimizes wrapping.
- Use native table objects, short headers, subtle horizontal rules, and aligned numeric columns.
- Highlight only the comparison that matters. Avoid coloring every cell.

## Timeline cards

Use for 3-6 ordered stages, dated events, or decision gates.

- Use a horizontal timeline for up to five short events.
- Use a vertical timeline when descriptions are longer or dates vary in width.
- Fields: date or stage, event label, one-line outcome or decision.
- Keep connectors behind text and preserve chronological spacing when time intervals matter.

## Risks and next steps

Use when each risk has a response, owner, or immediate action.

- Layout: two columns labeled `Risks` and `Next steps`, or paired rows when each action maps to one risk.
- Fields: specific risk, impact, action, owner or timing when supplied.
- Use warning color only on the risk marker. Keep actions in the normal accent color.

## Density limits

- Prefer 2-4 cards per slide.
- Keep card body copy to one short supporting line when possible.
- Convert repeated prose to a table when it reduces duplication.
- If readable 17 pt text still does not fit, ask for prioritization or split the content only with the user's approval.
